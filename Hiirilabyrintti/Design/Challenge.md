# Challenge

## Mouse trap
Our game will have a Mouse trap. It will not make dmg or player has no ability to die. It will make our player character "Mouse" to drop some cheese.

Cheese is one of way to get out maze. And traps will make it slower. Also there is a drill and escape exit ways.

Lets say you need 10 cheese to "win". And there are 15 cheeses in level. Even if you hit all trap the traps and lose all cheeses, you still have two more ways to get out.

So no loser in this game.
